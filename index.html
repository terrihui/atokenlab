About Us 關於我們

A Token Lab | About Us A Token Lab｜關於我們

Limited Tokens. Unlimited Creation. 學之一時，用之一生

Truly valuable learning does not lie in short-term memorization of theory, but in internalizing knowledge, applying it meaningfully, and turning it into lifelong ability that students can carry with them through different stages of life.

真正有價值的學習，不在短暫的理論背誦，而在將知識內化、活用，成為學生終生受用的能力，應用於人生不同階段。

A Token Lab is a forward-looking, guided learning space that brings together STEM, artificial intelligence, and hands-on practice. At the heart of our teaching is helping students move from simply having "learned something" to truly understanding it, connecting knowledge with the real world through experimentation, problem-solving, and creation.

A Token Lab 打造前瞻引導式學習空間，融合 STEM、人工智能與動手實作。我們教學的核心，是讓學生從「學過」變成「學懂」，打通知識與現實的連結，透過實驗探索、解難與創作，真正活用所學。

👨‍🔬

Our educational philosophy is rooted in the founder's engineering background and industry experience. The founder graduated from the Department of Electronic and Communication Engineering at City University of Hong Kong in 2006, building a strong foundation in technical knowledge and systems thinking. After graduation, the founder developed extensive experience in industry, taking part in electronic procurement, product development, production coordination, and quality management, with a full understanding of the complete journey from concept to finished product.

本機構教學理念，源自創辦人的工程專業與產業實戰經驗。創辦人2006年畢業於香港城市大學電子及通訊工程系，具備扎實的技術與系統思維。畢業後深耕產業，參與電子採購、產品研發、生產與品管全流程，熟悉從概念到成品落地的完整運作。

With this practical background, the founder firmly believes that systematic thinking, rigorous logic, and hands-on ability are among the most essential strengths students need for the future.

憑藉豐富業界經驗，創辦人確信：系統化思維、嚴謹邏輯、動手實作能力，是學生未來最核心的競爭力。

Since 2017, the founder has focused on STEM education for primary and secondary school students, transforming professional engineering thinking into step-by-step learning that is clear and accessible. Since 2025, AI education has also been introduced, guiding students to use artificial intelligence as a tool for thinking, innovation, and expression while keeping pace with the digital age.

2017年起，創辦人專注中小學STEM教學，將專業工程思維轉化為淺白易懂的循序課程；2025年起，導入AI教學，指導學生善用人工智能作為思考、創新、表達的工具，緊貼數碼時代趨勢。

A Token Lab is more than a tutoring classroom. It is a future-focused growth lab that nurtures students' logic, ability, and confidence.

A Token Lab 不只是補習課室，更是培育學生邏輯、實力與自信的未來成長實驗室。

Our Vision 我們的願景

We aim to move beyond traditional passive learning and nurture students who are curious, independent in thought, and confident in practice. By balancing technological logic with creative expression, we help students build the strength and confidence to face the future with readiness and resilience.

突破傳統被動學習模式，培養學生主動探索、獨立思考、勇於實踐的特質。平衡科技邏輯與創意表達，讓學生累積底氣與實力，從容迎向未來挑戰。
